IRSA Faragher Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellow

I develop statistical methods and efficient algorithms for learning from complex, high-dimensional data.

My work connects machine learning, optimization, and statistical computation, with a focus on quantile regression and imbalanced classification. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Iowa, advised by Boxiang Wang.
