Qian Tang

I develop statistical methods and efficient algorithms for learning from complex modern data.

I am an IRSA Faragher Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellow in the School of Statistics at the University of Minnesota. Previously, I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the University of Iowa, advised by Boxiang Wang. Before that, I earned an M.S. in Statistics from the University of California, Davis and a B.S. in Information and Computation Science from Jilin University.
